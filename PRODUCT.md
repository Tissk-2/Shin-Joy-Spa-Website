# Shin Joy Spa

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors browse spa treatments in Indonesian or English, compare prices, and request an appointment. Signed-in customers can review their bookings. Authorized staff manage bookings and the published catalog.

## Product Purpose

A Laravel/Inertia website for Shin Joy Spa with a database-backed treatment catalog and staff-approved booking requests. Every new booking is paid at the spa; online payment is deferred to a later release. Bookings are not rescheduled: customers ask the spa to cancel and make a new booking. Room scheduling, Telegram staff notifications and actions, and customer status emails are built, and each stays off until the spa switches it on.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, welcoming. Preserve the existing rose, gold, dark brown palette and serif-heading/body-text pairing, as requested in the September 2026 review.

## Anti-references

Avoid homepage treatment cards that behave like full booking forms, hidden content dependent on animation initialization, unlabelled duplicate mobile actions, and low-contrast VIP headings. Do not replace the established identity with an unrelated design style.

## Design Principles

- Let visitors browse before asking them to configure a booking.
- Keep treatment configuration on the detail and booking pages.
- Preserve server-authoritative prices and historical booking snapshots.
- Use the supplied Nezuko and Nezuko VIP menus as the pricing reference.
- Treat motion as enhancement; content remains visible without it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA text contrast, visible keyboard focus, keyboard-operable treatment choices, reduced-motion support, and responsive controls with at least 44px touch targets. Keep currency and its amount together.
